Privacy

Last updated: 13 November 2026 · FS AI (FAHAD SOMRAT AI), founded by Fahad Somrat.

Short version

FS AI is a zero-cost platform. It collects almost nothing, sells no data, runs no paid services, and shows no ads yet. Reading articles and using the 20 free tools needs no account and sends nothing anywhere — every calculation happens in your browser.

What is collected

  • Analytics (GA4, Measurement ID G-JVSCJT2DHJ). This site uses Google Analytics 4, which records anonymous, aggregated usage (pages visited, approximate location, device type). No names, emails or account details are sent to Analytics by this site. Traffic figures are reported honestly as “Data unavailable” until a real GA4 report is wired into the dashboard — no visitor, click or earnings numbers are invented.
  • Newsletter (only if you subscribe). If you use the subscribe form, your email address (and your name, if you give one) is stored in the FS AI database as pending confirmation. Confirmation emails cannot be sent yet: no free email sender is connected. This is not double opt-in, and no confirmation email will arrive until a free sender is set up. You can unsubscribe at any time from the form on the home page; unsubscribing marks the address unsubscribed.
  • Owner sign-in (Command Center only). The owner’s email and password are checked by the FS AI server. Passwords are stored only as a PBKDF2 hash on the server, never in plain text and never in frontend code. A short-lived session token is kept in your browser’s session storage for that tab only.
  • Your browser only. Your theme choice (dark/light) is stored in your own browser’s local storage. Tool inputs are never sent anywhere.

What is never done

  • No personal data is sold, shared or rented to anyone.
  • No personal or family content is used on this platform.
  • No paid services are used to run this site, and no payment details are collected.
  • No account is created for readers. There is no tracking across other sites.

Third parties

Pages are served by Cloudflare Pages and the API runs on Cloudflare Workers (free tier). Google Analytics (GA4) is the only third-party analytics service. GitHub hosts the public source repository; YouTube and Pinterest are linked accounts only — this site does not connect to them via API and nothing is posted automatically.

Your choices

You can block Analytics with a browser setting or extension, clear the stored theme choice from your browser, and unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time. To ask about data held about you, contact the site using the address on the home page footer (mdalfahad841@gmail.com).

Changes

If this policy changes, the “Last updated” date above changes with it. The site will never quietly start selling data or charging readers — that would break the zero-cost promise this platform is built on.
