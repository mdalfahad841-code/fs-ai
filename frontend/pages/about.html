About FS AI

FS AI — FAHAD SOMRAT AI. Founded by Fahad Somrat. “One Platform. Multiple Possibilities.”

What this is

FS AI is a multi-niche digital platform: 25 articles across 8 divisions — Automotive, AI & Technology, Gadgets & Electronics, Finance & Money Education, Travel & Lifestyle, Digital Tools & Utilities, Health & Wellness, and Education & Learning — plus 20 free tools (converters and calculators) that run entirely in your browser.

Zero-cost, honest numbers

This platform runs on free services only. There is no paid hosting, no paid subscription and no advertising yet, so revenue is honestly $0.00. Traffic is reported as “Data unavailable” until a real analytics report is connected — no visitor, click or earnings numbers are invented anywhere on this site.

How it is run

A 16-agent AI business management system handles research, content, fact-checking, SEO, publishing, analytics and security, with publishing only after the owner’s approval. Nothing is posted automatically to any social account. The source code is public on GitHub.

What this is not

  • No personal or family content is used on this platform.
  • No account is needed to read articles or use the tools.
  • Health and finance content is general education only — it is not medical or financial advice.
