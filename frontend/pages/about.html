About FS AI

FS AI — FAHAD SOMRAT AI. Founded by Fahad Somrat. “One Platform. Multiple Possibilities.”

What this is

FS AI is a multi-niche digital platform: free articles across every division — Automotive, AI & Technology, Gadgets & Electronics, Finance & Money Education, Travel & Lifestyle, Digital Tools & Utilities, Health & Wellness, and Education & Learning — plus free tools (converters and calculators) that run entirely in your browser.

Zero-cost

This platform runs on free services only. There is no paid hosting, no paid subscription and no advertising yet. All content is free to read.

How it is run

Articles are researched, drafted, fact-checked and published with AI assistance, with publishing only after the owner’s approval. Nothing is posted automatically to any social account. The source code is public on GitHub.

What this is not

  • No personal or family content is used on this platform.
  • No account is needed to read articles or use the tools.
  • Health and finance content is general education only — it is not medical or financial advice.
